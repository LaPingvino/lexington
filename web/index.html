Lexington

Convert a screenplay: Fountain, Final Draft (FDX) or a PDF to PDF, Word, OpenDocument, HTML, Final Draft, Fountain or LaTeX. It all happens in your browser; your script is not sent anywhere.

Have a screenplay only as a PDF, even a scanned one? You can use it: here the PDF's text is read, and for scanned pages, give the version on your computer a try, Accolade or the lexington command, which read them with OCR. They also make EPUB, Kindle books, Markdown and more.
